Architecture

How the pieces fit together

1. Capture

Packets from a mirror port or TAP, or flows from your routers. PF_RING and Cento handle the high speeds.

2. Analyze

nProbe turns packets into flows, nDPI labels every application, and ntopng shows hosts, flows and alerts.

3. Record and ask

n2disk keeps the packets as proof, and nAnalyst answers questions about everything it sees.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What features does ntopng include for free?

The Community edition includes real-time visibility and top talkers, Layer-7 application detection with nDPI, alerts, timeseries and network discovery. It is open source under GPLv3.

Do I need all the ntop products?

No. Start with ntopng alone. Add nProbe when your routers export NetFlow, n2disk when you need recorded packets, and nAnalyst when you want AI-assisted investigation.

Does my traffic leave my network?

No. ntop software is self-hosted and the nAnalyst AI assistant is designed to run on-premises.

Can it handle high-speed networks?

Yes. With PF_RING and nProbe Cento, ntopng analyzes traffic on 10 to 100 Gbit links, and n2disk can record up to 100 Gbit.
