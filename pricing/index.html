ntopng costs nothing to start

How much does ntopng cost?

The Community edition is free. Pro and Enterprise editions are commercial licenses sized to your network. Licenses are perpetual and include one year of maintenance, updates and support. Educational users can get commercial products at no cost. See current licenses in the shop or ask for a quote.

Other products are sold as perpetual licenses in the ntop shop, and appliances on quote. For volume, OEM or integrator needs, ask for a quote.

Quick guide

Which product do I need?

I want to see my traffic

ntopng shows hosts, flows and applications.

My routers export NetFlow or sFlow

nProbe collects the flows for ntopng.

I need 10 to 100 Gbit flows

nProbe Cento handles high-speed links.

I need the packets as proof

n2disk records and indexes traffic.

I need DDoS protection

nScrub filters attacks at line rate.

I want hardware that just works

nBox appliances ship preconfigured.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is ntopng licensed?

The Community edition is free and open source. Pro and Enterprise editions are commercial, with perpetual licenses that include one year of maintenance, updates and support. See the shop for current licenses.

How is nProbe licensed?

nProbe is available in Pro and Enterprise editions, and nProbe Cento in several sizes for 10 to 100 Gbit links. Licenses are sold in the ntop shop.

Is ntopng free?

Yes. The Community edition of ntopng is free and open source under the GNU GPLv3 license. Pro and Enterprise editions are commercial.

Are the licenses perpetual?

Yes. All ntop software licenses in the shop are perpetual. They include installation support, one year of maintenance, updates and support by email or community channels.

Is there a free license for education?

Yes. Educational users can obtain commercial products at no cost from the ntop shop.

Which ntop products are free?

ntopng Community, nDPI and PF_RING are free and open source.

How do I buy an nBox appliance?

nBox Mini is sold through a partner store. nBox NetFlow and nBox Recorder are sold on quote, contact us with your link speed.

Can I try a commercial product before buying?

Install the free Community edition first and talk to the ntop team about trials of commercial editions such as nAnalyst.
