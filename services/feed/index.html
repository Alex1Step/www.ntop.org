ntop is – and will always remain – open source software. ntop is licensed under the GPL license, which is available at the Free Software Foundation’s web site.

ntop community support is available on a best-efforts basis as described here. Despite many individual’s frequent postings, nobody is responsible for answering your question. Responses may be non-existent, incomplete or inaccurate. We recognize that this may not be appropriate for deployment of ntop in a commercial enterprise. And we are aware that open source application like ntop are not deployed without commercial support.

As one of our goals is to spread adoption of ntop and to eliminate barriers, we offer premium, fee-based services directly from the ntop developers, including:

  • Consultancy Services (Implementation, deployment, analysis of data)
  • Commercial-grade Support (phone, email, or on-site)
  • Professional Training
  • Customization of ntop for unique needs

If you are interested in one or more of these services please contact us.

Master ntop Tools with Hands-On Guidance

Learn directly from the creators of ntop through structured, hands-on training sessions. Our courses cover traffic analysis, DPI, flow monitoring, packet capture, and performance optimization. Tailored for both enterprise and service provider environments.

ntop offers professional training in addition to periodic webinars, video tutorials, and community support.

ntop training mainly focus on ntopng, that leverages on most ntop tools, ranging from DPI, to packet capture and traffic analysis. Custom-tailored training content focusing on other ntop tools is offered as professional services.

Training topics Include:

  • Network traffic monitoring with ntopng and nProbe
  • Flow export and IPFIX customization
  • Deep packet inspection with nDPI
  • High-speed packet capture with PF_RING and n2disk
  • DDoS detection and mitigation strategies
  • Real-world deployment and troubleshooting labs

Training Contents

The training course lasts 6 hours and it is in English language. It is provided through the Microsoft Teams platform and it is divided in six parts broadcasted across two weeks. Attendees should have a basic networking knowledge in order to attend the training. The training package includes (free of charge) a full ntopng Pro license to be used during and after the training.

Program (subject to minor changes based on the latest software features and releases):

Part I (90 minutes) – Introduction

  • Introduction to ntopng: main features and problems that can be tackled with it
  • ntopng architecture: how it works, main components, interaction with external applications
  • Traffic Analysis
    • Packets and Deep Packet Inspection (mirror ports, SPAN, TAP, nTAP)
    • Flow collection (sFlow, NetFlow, IPFIX)
    • Packets vs flows: discussion, pros and cons
  • Supported platforms and operating systems
  • ntopng installation
    • Linux (Ubuntu, Rocky Linux, …)
    • Windows
    • FreeBSD/OPNsense/pfSense
  • Licenses
    • License model
    • Differences between versions (community vs Pro vs Enterprise M/L/…)
    • Maintenance
    • The system ID
    • Create and apply licenses
    • Licensing VMs and containers (e.g. Docker)
  • Service configuration
    • Systemd and configuration files

Part II (90 minutes) – Network Intelligence

  • Ping google.com
    • What does ntopng see?
    • Active hosts and flows, their lifecycle
  • Alerts, anomalies, network and security issues
    • Checks and check configuration
    • Exclusion lists
  • Analyzing a pcap file with bad DNS traffic
    • Alerts and associated risks
  • The score indicator of compromise
  • Delivering alerts to external recipients (mail, Discord, Telegram, Slack, ElasticSearch)
    • Endpoint and Recipients
    • Delivering alerts to a Discord channel

Part III (90 minutes) – Flow Collection

  • Flow collection vs packet collection: pros and cons, sFlow and NetFlow/IPFIX
  • How to collect flows sFlow and NetFlow/IPFIX. Integration of nProbe with ntopng
  • Collecting NetFlow from nProbe
    • Configure nProbe to export NetFlow
    • Configure nProbe to collect NetFlow
    • Connecting ntopng with nProbe
  • Flow collection behind NAT
    • Collector and probe mode
  • Collecting from large sets of devices: Observation Points
  • Continuous PCAP Recording
    • Drill down from charts and alerts to raw packets
  • Hardware sizing, performances and tuning

Part IV (90 minutes) – Historical Data

  • Historical data
    • Timeseries
    • Flows
    • Alerts
  • Historical data configuration
    • Timeseries for interfaces and local hosts
  • Timeseries data stores (RRD, ClickHouse, Influxdb)
  • Flow data stores (ClickHouse, ElasticSearch)
  • Looking at how data is stored on disk
    • The data directory
  • Accessing historical data
    • Timeseries and flow explorer
    • Packets and data drill down (n2disk)
  • Data retention
  • Sizing

Part V (90 minutes) – Active Monitoring, SNMP, Autonomous Systems

  • Network monitoring polling SNMP devices (switches and routers)
  • LLDP and network topology
  • Active monitoring (ICMP, Continuous ICMP, Speedtest, HTTP, throughput)
    • Monitor the infrastructure health
    • Get notified about the status of services
    • React: running custom actions
    • Vulnerability Scan
  • Autonomous Systems traffic monitoring
  • Open discussion: Q&A
  • A certificate of proficiency (not attendance) can be requested and provided upon an exam run by the ntop team that verifies that you qualify for it.


Part VI (90 minutes) – AI-Assisted Analysis

  • Introduction to nAnalyst
    • nAnalyst vs traditional ntopng analysis vs generic AI assistants
  • Architecture and data flow, LLM against ntopng data
  • Getting started and configuration
    • Configuring the LLM provider
    • Cloud vs local models
    • Model selection
    • Permission Model
  • Asking Questions: from simple to advanced
    • Prompting techniques for network analysis
    • What Is Happening on My Network?
    • Security & Incident Investigation with interactive charting
  • AI policies: from analysis to automation
    • Converting a natural-language requirement into a detection policy and alerts
  • AI powered, grafana style, custom dashboards
  • Advanced Integration with MCP
    • Exposing nAnalyst capabilities to external AI clients (e.g. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor)
