]]> Is your network monitoring keeping pace with AI, larger infrastructures, and the growing need for real observability? We’re excited to introduce ntopng 7.0, alongside new releases of the rest of the ntop suite.

This is one of the biggest ntopng releases to date: a new AI-assisted analysis engine, native SIEM integration, a network-to-site hierarchy for large deployments, BGP visibility, a completely rebuilt observability/timeseries backend on ClickHouse, and a modernized Vue.js frontend. We want to walk you through it live, with a Q&A session at the end of the event.

Here is what we will cover:
  • nAnalyst — AI-assisted traffic analysis. A brand-new chat-based assistant that lets you query live and historical flows and alerts in different languages, define AI-driven alert policies, and get AI-generated dashboards, all through an agentic loop that supports multiple LLM providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Qwen, and local models) plus a built-in MCP server for connecting external AI agents to ntopng.
  • Wazuh XDR/SIEM integration. Native collection and correlation of Wazuh alerts into ClickHouse, with asset enrichment, dedicated alert pages, MITRE-mapped rules, and configurable retention, bringing SIEM-grade security visibility directly into ntopng.
  • Sites: hierarchy for large networks. A new network-to-site model designed for ISPs, MSPs, and large infrastructures, with a sites dashboard, geomap, matrix/heatmap views, and per-site timeseries and statistics.
  • BGP & BMP support. A new Looking Glass page, RPKI validation, prefix-change alerts, and full BGP context surfaced directly in live and historical flows, a big step for routing observability.
  • A rebuilt Observability backend. A new ClickHouse-based timeseries engine, including a High-Resolution counters driver, a redesigned Observability dashboard, and out-of-the-box Grafana integration.
  • Modern authentication & a new frontend. SSO via OpenID Connect, Multi-Factor Authentication, Passkeys, and API key support, alongside a full migration to a Vue.js frontend with D3-based charts replacing the legacy UI.
  • Plus: user-defined host Tags, automatic flow deduplication, extended OT visibility (new S7comm and Profinet support), the new nEdge Lite, and a long list of performance and security hardening improvements across the board.
Who should attend?
  • Network engineers and SREs who want deeper, AI-assisted visibility into their infrastructure.
  • Security and SOC teams looking to leverage the new Wazuh integration and AI-driven alerting.
  • ISPs, service providers, and enterprises managing large, multi-site networks or their own Autonomous Systems.
  • Existing ntopng users who want a guided tour of what’s new before upgrading.
Secure your spot

The event will be recorded and shared with our community for those who cannot attend live.

Looking forward to seeing you there!

Artificial intelligence is now a mature technology that is changing everyday life. Observability and cybersecurity are not an exception. This webinar explains the ntop vision on using AI, what we have done, and what is the direction we have in mind.

]]> Is your network a “black box,” or do you have the granular clarity needed to stay ahead of modern traffic demands?

We are excited to announce an upcoming two-part webinar focused on the latest enhancements to nProbe and ntopng. These updates are designed to push the boundaries of what’s possible in network monitoring, moving beyond simple statistics into the realm of true, high-fidelity observability.

Here is what we will cover:

Part 1: High-Resolution Observability

Standard monitoring often misses the “micro-bursts” and transient issues that cause performance bottlenecks. We’ll demonstrate how our new enhancements allow you to observe network traffic at second resolution. This level of granularity ensures you can see everything—from subtle latency spikes to hidden traffic patterns—providing a detailed forensic view of your infrastructure in real-time.

Part 2: Routing & BGP for Large-Scale Networks

For ISPs, service providers, and organizations managing their own Autonomous Systems (AS), routing stability is everything. We are introducing dedicated features for BGP observability. We’ll show you how to leverage ntopng and nProbe to monitor routing health, visualize BGP paths, and gain actionable insights into how your traffic moves across the global Internet.

Who should attend?

  • Network Engineers and SREs looking for deeper visibility.
  • ISP and Service Provider operators managing complex BGP environments.
  • IT Managers who need to justify infrastructure performance with data.

PacketFest ’25 is a two-day (May 8th and 9th) technical conference in Zurich, Switzerland, bringing together the ntop and Wireshark communities. The event features presentations and workshops on network traffic monitoring, cybersecurity, and open-source technologies, with a focus on practical applications and the latest advancements in ntop and Wireshark tools. Attendees can expect interactive sessions, networking opportunities, and expert insights including the creators of ntop and Wireshark. A pre-conference (May 7th) ntop training day is also offered. Registration fees apply, but students and non-profits can attend free of charge.

Registration is now open !

PacketFest ’25 FAQ

What is PacketFest?

PacketFest is a two-day technical conference focused on network traffic monitoring, visibility, cybersecurity, and open-source technologies. It brings together the ntop and Wireshark communities to share knowledge, discuss challenges, and explore the latest advancements in these tools.

 

What are the key objectives of PacketFest ’25?

The main goals of PacketFest ’25 are:
• Providing practical solutions for real-world packet-related challenges.
• Showcasing the newest features in ntop and Wireshark.
• Fostering networking opportunities within the community.
• Inspiring attendees with insights from industry experts.
• Gathering valuable feedback for future development roadmaps.
• Encouraging active interaction and exchange of ideas.

 

When and where is PacketFest ’25 taking place?

PacketFest ’25 is held in Zurich, Switzerland. The main conference takes place on May 8th and 9th, with a pre-conference training day on May 7th.
• Conference Venue: PH Zürich, Lagerstrasse 2, 8090 Zürich, Switzerland
• Pre-Conference Venue: Switch, Werdstrasse 2, 8004 Zürich, Switzerland

Both venues are within walking distance of Zurich Hauptbahnhof (main train station).

 

What topics will be covered at PacketFest ’25?

The conference agenda covers a wide range of topics, including:
• Deep Packet Inspection (DPI)
• High-Speed Traffic Analysis and Monitoring
• Cybersecurity in IT and OT environments
• Network Anomaly Detection and Telemetry
• Traffic Classification and Obfuscated Flow Detection• SmartNICs and PF_RING utilization
• Artificial Intelligence in network analysis
• Open Source contributions and future development

 

Is there a pre-conference event, and what does it entail?

Yes, a pre-conference training day is scheduled for May 7th. It will feature hands-on workshops and sessions focusing on:
• Using ntop tools for cybersecurity.
• Integrating ntopng with Wazuh SIEM.
• Deep dives into ntopng and nDPI.
• Open Source OT monitoring.

What is the registration fee for PacketFest ’25?

Early bird registration (until Feb 28th) is 125 CHF. From March 1st, the fee is 150 CHF. The pre-conference event is included in the registration fee but has limited seating.

 

Are there any discounts or waivers available?

Yes, students (University, Hochschule, PhD) and non-profit organizations can attend free of charge. However, some restrictions may apply. Please contact the organizers via email for registration details.

 

Who are the organizers of PacketFest ’25?

PacketFest is organized by ntop, with support from Switch, AnyWeb, and Leutert NetServices.
