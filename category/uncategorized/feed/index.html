As we did in the past, this year we’re organizing the network devroom at FOSDEM that will take place in Brussels next week-end Sat and Sun February 1-2. Most of ntop team will be there and it will be a great to meet our community. This time we will be talking about nDPI and smart NICs.

Furthermore it is a pleasure to also host two invited speakers of popular open source projects:

  • Victor Julien the creator of Suricata IDS
  • Gerald Combs the creator of Wireshark

Looking forward meeting you at FOSDEM !
