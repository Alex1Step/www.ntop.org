The recommendation

Who should choose nBox NetFlow

nBox NetFlow is a turn-key NetFlow v5/v9/IPFIX probe in a 1U rackmount appliance, based on nProbe and nProbe Cento. Choose it when you want a probe without building and maintaining a PC, even at 100 Gbit.

  • You need a probe where space is limited and moving parts are a risk.
  • You want line rate analysis up to 100 Gbit with Layer-7 classification.
  • You want a hardware warranty: Dell-based systems include up to 3 years on-site.

The ideal use case

Ideal use case for nBox NetFlow

  • Environment: 19 inch racks, with models from 2.5 Gbit/s to 100 Gbit/s (L, M, H10/25/40, H100).
  • Team: teams that prefer to buy a ready appliance and manage it from the web interface.
  • Technical requirements: nProbe up to 10 Gbit, nProbe Cento up to 100 Gbit. ntopng is optional.
  • Problem solved: accurate flow export (NetFlow, Kafka, ClickHouse, ZMQ, CSV, JSON) with nDPI classification.

The alternatives

nBox NetFlow alternatives, compared

Same criteria for each option: role, when to choose it, limits and cost.

nProbe or nProbe Cento software
  • Role: Same software on your own hardware.
  • Choose it when: You already have suitable servers.
  • Limits: You size, install and maintain it.
  • Cost: from 299.95 euro
nBox Mini
  • Role: Low-cost appliance with ntopng preinstalled.
  • Choose it when: Smaller links with a dashboard included.
  • Limits: Up to 2.5 Gbit link speed on the models listed.
  • Cost: Ask the reseller

The tradeoffs

When nBox NetFlow is not the right choice

  • Price is by quote and depends on the model.
  • It is a probe: use ntopng or another collector for visibility.
  • Pick the model for your link speed up front.

Open Source Technologies

nBox relies on Open Source high-performance technologies for capturing and processing traffic, including our PF_RING framework and our nDPI Deep Packet Inspection framework, delivering Line-Rate packet processing up to 100 Gbit/s.

High Performance, Small Form Factor

Our nBox NetFlow and Recorder appliances are able to process up to 100 Gbps fully leveraging on multi-core architectures to scale with the performance, and fit in 1U form factor rackmount.
